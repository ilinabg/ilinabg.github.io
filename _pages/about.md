I am a postdoctoral scholar at Stanford University in the [Gwilliams Laboratory of Speech Neuroscience](https://gwilliamslab.stanford.edu/). I completed a PhD at UC Berkeley-UCSF in the [Chang lab](http://changlab.ucsf.edu/){:target="_blank"}, where I studied how long-term language experience affects the human brain's representations of speech sounds, sound sequences, and words. You can read (and hear!) more about my research on [this](/research) page. I was fortunate to have been supported by the [NSF Graduate Research Fellowship](https://www.nsfgrfp.org/){:target="_blank"}, the [H2H8 Fellowship](https://h2h8.com/){:target="_blank"}, and the [UCSF Discovery Fellowship](https://graduate.ucsf.edu/admission/financial-support/fellowships/discovery-fellows-program){:target="_blank"}.

As an undergraduate at UC Berkeley, I studied Cognitive Science and Computer Science. I was a research assistant in the [Knight lab](http://knightlab.neuro.berkeley.edu){:target="_blank"}, where I worked with [Arjen Stolk](https://pbs.dartmouth.edu/people/arjen-stolk){:target="_blank"} on a communication study focused on the neural correlates of non-verbal human communication. You can play the communication game used in this experiment [here](https://www.mutualunderstanding.nl/game){:target="_blank"}.

Outside of research, I enjoy public engagement, science communication, and the arts. Check out my work in this space on the [public engagement](/public-engagement) page. You can also read more about engagement projects I've been a part of at the [Kavli Center for Ethics, Science, and the Public website](https://kavlicenter.berkeley.edu/).

My CV can be found [here](files/cv_current.pdf){:target="_blank"} (updated October 2026). If you'd like to reach out, please contact me at ibg [at] berkeley.edu.
